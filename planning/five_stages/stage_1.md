# **Stage 1 — Codex-Only Alfas (No Embodiment)**

**Four Stages Doctrine · High Command**

**Status:** Active
**Purpose:** Establish safe Alfa generation and prohibit embodiment
**Scope:** All ten genesis workspaces

---

## **1. Mission**

Stage 1 defines the only allowed mode of Alfa creation during early system development:

### **All Alfas must be generated in Codex, not embodied in the battlegrid.**

This ensures:

* sandbox execution
* human approval
* deterministic behavior
* no rituals
* no workflows
* no autonomous cascades

Codex acts as the safety cage until the system matures.

---

## **2. Why Codex-Only?**

Codex Alfas are:

* non-embodied
* approval-gated
* contract-bound
* unable to run free
* unable to trigger rituals or workflows
* safe by construction

By contrast, **embodied Alfas** (battlegrid agents):

* can execute rituals
* can manipulate state
* can trigger workflows
* can cause cascading effects

Embodiment requires a **much higher safety tier**.

---

## **3. Mandatory Rules (Stage 1)**

### **Rule 1 — No Battlegrid**

No Alfa may run, be summoned, or act inside any battlegrid UI.

### **Rule 2 — No Ritual Execution**

No Alfa may execute spells, migrations, syncs, or schema-manipulation rituals.

### **Rule 3 — Codex Only**

All Alfas must be:

* written by Codex
* reviewed by a human
* executed inside the CLI sandbox

### **Rule 4 — Prepare for Delta**

Each workspace must accumulate **16 safe Codex-generated Alfas** before Stage 2 becomes available.

---

## **4. Transition Criteria to Stage 2**

High Command may authorize Stage 2 once:

* each workspace reaches **Delta (16 Alfas)**
* all Alfas are documented
* Toyfoundry validates their contract compliance
* Toysoldiers runs readiness checks
* Valiant Citadel verifies safety
* ledger entries remain consistent

Only then may Azure Cloud deployment begin.

---

## **5. Summary**

Stage 1 establishes the safety-first foundation of the Four Stages Doctrine:

* **Codex generates.**
* **No embodiment.**
* **No battlegrid.**
* **Build Deltas first.**
* **Safety before spectacle.**

This stage remains in force until High Command authorizes Stage 2.
