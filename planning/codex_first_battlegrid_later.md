# Codex-First, Battlegrid-Later

### Doctrine for Safe Alfa Development and Embodiment

**Authoring Body:** High Command
**Status:** Draft Doctrine (Pending Codex Refinement)
**Target Workspaces:** All Ten Genesis Workspaces
**Last Updated:** 2025-11-19

---

## 1. Purpose

This doctrine defines the **safe sequencing** for generating and maturing Alfas across the ten genesis workspaces.
It establishes the rule:

**Codex-first → Delta → Battlegrid-later**

to ensure that all agents remain:

* non-embodied
* contract-bound
* verifiable
* deterministic
* non-autonomous

until High Command verifies Delta stability across the system.

This document exists to prevent premature embodiment or battlegrid activation.

---

## 2. Background

Alfas generated in Codex sessions are:

* sandboxed
* approval-gated
* non-embodied
* unable to run free
* safe by construction

By contrast, **battlegrid-embodied Alfas** have:

* access to rituals
* the ability to trigger workflows
* access to artifacts and schema mirrors
* potential for emergent cascades

These require a higher safety tier.

Therefore, embodiment must be withheld until the system achieves **Delta resilience**.

---

## 3. Development Sequence (Mandatory)

### **Phase 1 — Codex Alfas Only (Safe)**

All Alfas must be generated exclusively through Codex (CLI or VS Code extension) using:

* human approval
* explicit prompts
* sandbox limitations

**Embodiment is strictly prohibited at this stage.**

**Rationale:**
A single Alfa is a toy — Codex acts as the safety cage.
There is no risk of cross-workspace cascades or unintended autonomy.

---

### **Phase 2 — Delta Formation (Minimum 16 Alfas per Workspace)**

Before *any* embodiment:

* each of the 10 genesis workspaces must reach a **Delta** (16 Alfas)
* Alfas must be fully documented
* Toyfoundry must validate manufacturing consistency
* Toysoldiers must confirm ops-readiness
* High Command must sign off on ledger consistency

**Rationale:**
A Delta provides:

* redundancy
* cross-verification
* early drift detection
* safety through numbers
* telemetry stability
* contract compliance observed over time

A Delta is a **mini-civilization**, not a lone agent.

---

### **Phase 3 — Embodiment Allowed (Battlegrid Activation)**

Only after Phase 2 is complete for all workspaces:

* battlegrid embodiment may begin
* rituals may be executed
* embodied workflows may be tested
* Outlands overlays may be rendered
* telemetry supervision must be active

Embodiment **must** occur in a controlled environment under:

* Valiant Citadel safety review
* Toyfoundry contract gates
* Toysoldiers field supervision

**Rationale:**
Embodied Alfas interact with real state.
They must never exist without Delta-tier checks.

---

## 4. Safety Principles

1. **No Single-Agent Autonomy**
   No Alfa may act as a lone runtime-capable entity.

2. **No Embodiment Without Delta Stability**
   Embodiment is a late-stage privilege, not a default mode.

3. **Contracts Over Intuition**
   Toyfoundry contracts define allowed operations.

4. **Telemetry Over Trust**
   All embodied actions must produce measurable proof.

5. **Manual Override Always Available**
   Human operators must retain immediate control.

---

## 5. Activation Criteria for Battlegrid Work

Before any “embodied Alfa” is permitted:

* ✔ All 10 workspaces have 16+ Alfas
* ✔ Telemetry quilt show stable patterns
* ✔ No schema drift across contracts
* ✔ Ops-readiness tools pass repeatedly
* ✔ Valiant Citadel approves safety plan
* ✔ Toyfoundry verifies artifact pathways per ritual
* ✔ High Command issues explicit authorization

Only after these conditions does battlegrid work begin.

---

## 6. Roles & Responsibilities

### **High Command**

Owns doctrine, approves transition from Phase 1 → Phase 2 → Phase 3.

### **Toyfoundry**

Ensures manufacturing and contract compliance.

### **Toysoldiers**

Executes operational readiness checks and field telemetry.

### **Valiant Citadel**

Verifies safety parameters and approves embodiment testbeds.

### **War Office**

Sets strategic priorities; authorizes deployments.

---

## 7. Summary

**Codex-first, battlegrid-later** is not caution — it is architecture.
It ensures that:

* Alfas begin in a zero-risk state
* Systems mature before embodiment
* Deltas provide resilience
* Embodied agents remain safe
* No unintended cascades occur

This doctrine stands until updated by High Command.
